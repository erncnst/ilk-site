About

Capturing what light
reveals about people.

I work with available light whenever possible — not because it is easier, but because it is honest. It shows people as they are, in moments that would have passed without a camera.

My work has appeared in Vogue Turkey, Elle, and various editorial publications across Europe. I am based in Istanbul and available for travel worldwide.
